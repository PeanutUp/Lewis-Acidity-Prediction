Please consider “Coordination Constraints and Information Content in Predicting Lewis Acidity” as an Article for the *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling*.

This work examines what geometric representations encode about fluoride binding and how to tell when a predictor needs explicit information about the bound state. On FIA49k, we compare directional message passing with released connectivity predictions on the same molecules and evaluate classical descriptors, electronic quantities, and geometry controls. We relate the differences in error to denticity and accepting-center chemistry, and use a separate ligand-scaffold-combination holdout to assess transfer. Testing the same fitted model on paired geometries further identifies a source of error tied to structural disagreement.

The analysis provides a chemical basis for choosing a representation and deciding when to use a quantum refinement workflow. The accompanying code, frozen predictions, and model configurations allow these comparisons to be inspected at the molecule level.
